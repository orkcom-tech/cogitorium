# Code of conduct

Short, because it is not complicated.

**Be useful and be straight with people.** Say what you mean about the code.
Disagree with the work, not the person. Assume the other side is trying.

**Not welcome:** personal attacks, harassment of any kind, sustained
disruption, or bringing somebody's identity into a technical argument.

**If something happens** — to you or to somebody else — tell a maintainer
privately rather than in the thread. GitHub's
[private report form](https://github.com/orkcom-tech/cogitorium/security/advisories/new)
reaches us and nobody else, and it is fine to use it for this even though it
says security. Nothing is repeated further without asking you first. We will
read it, we will answer, and we will say what we did.

Consequences are proportionate and range from being asked to stop, through
having a comment removed, to being blocked from the organisation's
repositories.

This applies in issues, pull requests, commit messages, and anywhere else this
project is being discussed on our behalf.
